Our Story

Born from a simple frustration: why should renting a vehicle be complicated and expensive? DriveHive started in 2023 with a vision to transform India's vehicle rental landscape.

2023

The Beginning

Started with 5 vehicles in Hyderabad, focusing on transparency and customer experience.

2024

Rapid Growth

Expanded to 15+ cities, 500+ vehicles, and served over 10,000 happy customers.

2025

Innovation Focus

Launched EV fleet, AI-powered recommendations, and enhanced safety features.
